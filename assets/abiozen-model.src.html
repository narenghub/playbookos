What ties it together
The agent layer

Our coordination layer reads demand as it appears, matches it against what our suppliers hold, and routes the sourcing, the documentation and the testing as one piece of work rather than three.

It is why a request becomes a specified enquiry rather than a general one — and why the enquiry reaches you with the grade, quantity and timing already attached.

What this means if you supply material

  • Qualified enquiries, not catalogue requests. Demand arrives already specified, so the first exchange is about grade, quantity and lead time rather than discovery.
  • The documentation burden is shared. Compliance paperwork travels with the molecule instead of landing on you after the order.
  • Testing is arranged, not left open. Analysis is validated where the client’s reviewers are, which is usually what delays qualification.
  • One commercial relationship covering material, documentation and testing, rather than three to coordinate.

What this means if you buy material

  • Several hundred products across our supplier network, with sourcing as the thing we are fastest at.
  • Short a molecule? A supplier slipping, a qualification falling through, a volume you cannot cover — send it to us and we will say honestly whether we can help, and how quickly.
  • Material and paperwork together, so a programme is not held up waiting for documentation that should have arrived with the shipment.

What would help us move quickly

  1. Your full product list — the complete catalogue, not only what we discussed. We add demand continuously and would rather match against everything you make.
  2. Where a DMF is on file, and the grades you can supply today versus on request.
  3. Indicative lead times and minimum quantities, so we can answer a buyer without coming back to you first.
